What happens to the tokens

Escrow has exactly two doors. Tokens locked by the router can only leave towards a Node that paid for them, or back to you. There is no admin path to them at any fee, and settlement is one atomic transaction — there is no state where one leg happened and the other did not.

The line

An exit that closed with nothing above its floor keeps its tokens and takes a public place in line. It goes on taking bids there — but while an asset has a queue, only the exit at its head can settle.

Nobody buys their way past a seller who has been waiting. There is no function anywhere that reorders the line, not for the admin either — positions only ever change because the exit in front left.
